FEEDBACK LOG — Project 2 prototype
Harsh Menaria | Bike demand across time and weather

Feedback received — Raz and Maria (instructors):
The sequential colors were not distinct enough, and the monthly
line chart needed to distinguish the two years more clearly.

Changes completed in this prototype (after feedback):
I changed 2011 to tungsten black and 2012 to orange, added
circle/triangle scatter marks and a shared Year filter, and kept
one monthly line per year. The six weather bars are solid black
with visible numbers. I checked grayscale and preserved the old
screenshots alongside the revised ones.

My own reflection — planned version 3, after this prototype:
Reflecting on the project's purpose, I noticed I can do more with
the data to develop one clearer overall insight. Next, I plan to use
grouped daily averages by year, weather and rider type to connect
the views around demand growth. The existing data supports this;
I will explore additional compatible data if it adds useful context.
I will also review filtered-view readability and test usability.

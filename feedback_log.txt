Harsh Menaria | Bike demand across time and weather

Feedback received in class from Raz and Maria (instructors):
The sequential colors were not distinct enough, and the monthly
line chart needed to distinguish the two years more clearly.

Changes already made:
I acted on this feedback during the prototype stage because it
mainly concerned color and shape. I changed 2011 to tungsten black

and made the Year filter shared. The monthly chart keeps one line
per year. The weather chart retains its six original mean bars,
now solid black with visible numbers. I inspected the revision in
grayscale. The earlier screenshots are preserved beside new ones.

Planned before the final:
Review overlap and label readability in filtered views, keep the
